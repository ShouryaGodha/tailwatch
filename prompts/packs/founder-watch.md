READING
- "Stealth", "building something new", "Founder at Stealth" and a new company with the person as
  founder or CEO are founding. A side project alongside a full-time job is none unless the person
  says they left.
- A raise needs a named round, an amount or a lead investor in the evidence; rumours are none.
- A launch needs a public product, a waitlist or a launch post; a hiring post alone is none.
- One card per company event: the same raise reported by two sources is same_as the first.
priority is 0 to 100: a recent founding by a senior person with no announced raise ranks highest.
