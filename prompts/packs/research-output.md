WHAT COUNTS
- A paper counts once, at its first appearance. A v2 of an arXiv paper, a camera-ready version
  or the same paper appearing on another source is same_as the earlier alert.
- A repository counts when it is new and has substance (a README describing a project, code, a
  release). Forks, dotfiles, course homework and empty repositories are none.
- A release counts when it is a new major or minor version with notes; patch releases are minor.
- A post counts as an announcement only when the person says something new about their work or
  role in their own words. Reposts, likes and replies are none.

SUMMARY
The summary names the work ("Paper on sparse attention for long video, first author") and the
context line names the venue or the repository and its co-authors or organization when known.
priority is 0 to 100: the person's own lead work, wide relevance and novelty rank high.
