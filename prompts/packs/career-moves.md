WHAT IS NOT LOOKING
- A flag on its own: GitHub's hireable flag or LinkedIn's open-to-work flag, just switched on
  or already there. looking needs words from the person, within the last two weeks, saying
  they are looking, available or leaving; without them the answer is none, flags ["flag_only"].
- A state already there on the previous read: an open-to-work flag, an About line, a homepage
  sentence present in both reads. Only a change in this check can be new; what was already
  there is none, with same_as set to the earlier alert when one exists.
- Generic invitations: "open to interesting opportunities", "feel free to reach out", "happy to
  chat". looking needs an explicit search, stated availability, or a dated plan to move.
- A student or intern looking for an internship: none, category other, flags ["intern_seeker"].

WHAT IS NOT LEFT
- A new title or team at the same organization, a promotion, a rewording, commit activity, a
  re-uploaded CV, a post about work.
- A role listed through a staffing agency or vendor is the same role; reattributing it to the
  agency is not a departure. A part-time role held alongside the main job is not a destination.
- Volunteer roles, products listed as employers, courses and clubs are not employment.
- An account disappearing alone does not show a move. Missing fields and read errors never do.
- A role entry removed from a profile, while the previous read listed it as current and no read
  lists it any more, is a departure (left), with the uncertainty said in the reason.

READING TOGETHER
A current role at the same organization can coexist with an ended older one. Use evidence
timestamps: a profile fetched before an announcement does not contradict it by still showing
the old role. A report (a post or a feed entry naming the person) needs an identity match and
support in the evidence; say when it is hearsay. A first read can establish an existing search
or departure from the recorded organization; never treat old CV entries or historical posts as
new announcements because this is the first read. When no read lists the recorded organization
at all, do not say the person left it: the record may be wrong; say which employer the evidence
shows. A sentence that was removed is reported as "previously said ...", never as a current fact.

CATEGORY AND PRIORITY
Use the actual work. Researchers and engineers come first; data annotation and labelling roles
are out_of_scope unless the person's record says otherwise. priority is 0 to 100 within the
category: explicit current availability, a recent exit with no next role, strong relevant
experience and seniority rank high; an old completed move is weaker than a current search.
